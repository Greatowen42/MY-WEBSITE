5-Day High-Protein Meal Prep for Weight Loss (Easy Meal Prep for Work)

💪 35g Protein | 🔥 420 Calories | ⏱️ 2 Hrs Sunday Prep

Plan: 5 days · One main meal per day · Prep: about 2 hours on Sunday

Average per meal across the five meals below. Nutrition is an estimate and varies with brands.

Meal prep works best when every meal is simple, filling and different enough that you do not get bored. This plan pairs five Lean Table Recipes, each under 550 calories with at least 32g protein.

You cook once on Sunday, portion everything into containers, and each day's lunch is ready to grab. The five meals average about 420 calories and 35g protein, which leaves room for breakfast and snacks inside most weight loss calorie targets.

This plan covers one main meal a day. Add breakfast, snacks and sides to fit your own needs.

The 5 Meals

  • Monday: Chicken & Rice Power Bowl (32g protein, 510 calories)
  • Tuesday: Garlic Herb Turkey Meatballs (34g protein, 550 calories)
  • Wednesday: Sheet Pan Salmon & Veg (32g protein, 400 calories)
  • Thursday: One-Pot Turkey Chili (32g protein, 360 calories)
  • Friday: Greek Yogurt Chicken Cups (47g protein, 265 calories)

Each recipe page lists its own quantities, so scale them to the number of portions you need.

Shopping List Overview

  • Proteins: chicken, ground turkey, salmon, Greek yogurt
  • Vegetables: broccoli, green beans, asparagus, sweet potatoes, tomatoes, lettuce, onions, peppers
  • Pantry: brown rice, canned beans, canned tomatoes, olive oil, lemons, spices

Your Sunday Prep Order

  1. Read all five recipes once and check you have everything.
  2. Start the chili and the rice first. Both can bubble away on their own.
  3. While they cook, bake the turkey meatballs and the sheet pan salmon.
  4. Cook the chicken for the power bowl while the oven is busy.
  5. Let everything cool for 20 to 30 minutes before you close the lids.
  6. Portion Monday to Wednesday into containers for the fridge. Freeze Thursday's chili. Leave Friday's chicken cups for Wednesday evening, when the filling takes about 15 minutes to mix fresh.

Storage and Reheating

  • Fridge: cooked meals keep for 3 to 4 days. Monday to Wednesday stay in the fridge.
  • Freezer: freeze Thursday's chili. Move it to the fridge on Wednesday night to thaw.
  • Friday: mix the chicken salad fresh on Wednesday evening so it tastes its best.
  • Labels: write the day on each lid with masking tape.

Quick Questions

Can I prep a full 7 days? Yes, but freeze the later meals. The chili, meatballs and chicken bowl freeze well. The salmon and the chicken salad do not.

Is this plan good for weight loss? It gives you filling meals with at least 32g protein each. Weight loss depends on your total daily calories, so adjust portions to your own target.

Can I swap meals around? Yes. Keep the salmon and chicken salad early in the week, and put the chili and meatballs later.
