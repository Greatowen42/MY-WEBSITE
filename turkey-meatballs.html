Garlic Herb Turkey Meatball Meal Prep

Juicy, herb-infused baked turkey meatballs paired with roasted sweet potatoes and fluffy brown rice or quinoa. Perfect for healthy weekday lunches.

⏱ Prep Time: 15 mins 🔥 Cook Time: 20 mins 🍽 Servings: 4 meals 📊 410 Calories / meal

Ingredients

For the Turkey Meatballs:

  • 1.25 lbs [560g] lean ground turkey (93% lean recommended)
  • ½ cup [45g] rolled oats or panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup [15g] fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (dried oregano & thyme)
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • ½ tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (for drizzling)

For the Meal Prep Bowls:

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes [400g], diced
  • 2 cups [380g] cooked brown rice or quinoa
  • 3 cups [90g] fresh spinach or steamed broccoli
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, salt & pepper (for roasting veggies)

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Preheat & Prep

Preheat your oven to 400°F [200°C / Gas Mark 6]. Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. If preparing sweet potatoes, toss diced sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper, and spread onto one half of the tray or a separate baking sheet.

2

Mix & Shape Meatballs

In a large mixing bowl, combine ground turkey, oats/breadcrumbs, egg, minced garlic, parsley, Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper. Mix gently with your hands just until incorporated (do not overmix, or meatballs will become tough). Shape mixture into 16 equal meatballs.

3

Bake Until Golden

Place meatballs on the parchment-lined baking sheet spaced evenly apart. Lightly drizzle or spray with olive oil. Bake for 18–20 minutes until browned on top and cooked through (internal temperature reaches 165°F [74°C]).

4

Inspect & Glaze (Optional)

Remove from oven and allow to rest for 5 minutes. The meatballs should be juicy and golden-brown on the outside with visible herbs throughout.

5

Assemble Meal Prep Containers

Divide cooked brown rice, roasted sweet potatoes, and green vegetables evenly across 4 airtight meal prep containers. Add 4 turkey meatballs to each container. Cover tightly and store in the refrigerator for up to 4–5 days.
